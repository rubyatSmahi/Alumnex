## Sign in

Open `/login`, choose **Admin**, **Alumni**, or **Student** to fill a sample account, then submit the form. You can also enter any email and the shared demo password directly. Successful sign-in opens the matching role dashboard.

## Reset demo accounts

On the login page, select **Reset demo accounts**. This restores the original seeded users and clears the active demo session. Browser site data can also be cleared manually in developer tools.

## Registration

Students receive demo access immediately after registration. Alumni registrations are saved as pending and cannot sign in until approved. Public registration does not offer administrator accounts.
